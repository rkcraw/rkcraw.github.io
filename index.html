would the winds take into account your hair type,

or the rivers acknowledge how your skin differs from others?

some carry their rights like feathers,

light and unburdened,

while others are made to bear them like anchors,

dragging them down into that very river

we’re told to be responsible,

for upholding promise of freedom,

but what is freedom when it’s just a word,

can you belong to land,

when the land itself doesn’t know or care for your name?

do the trees whisper to you your rights,

does the heat from the sun trace your borders?]
